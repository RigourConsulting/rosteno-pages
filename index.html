Rosteno: Shift, Hours & Pay

Your shifts. Your hours. Your pay.

An independent, worker-owned Android app built for Australian workers to record rosters, actual shift times, confirmed breaks, and payslip comparisons.

About Rosteno

Rosteno helps Australian workers keep clear, reliable personal records of their working hours and pay. The app operates on a local-first basis, keeping your records on your phone.

Key Principles:
  • Australia-first: Designed around Australian working patterns and official Modern Award rules.
  • Local-first: Your records stay in Rosteno's app-private Android storage and Room database on your phone.
  • No employer portal: Rosteno is built for you, not your employer. Your records remain private.
  • No advertising: No ads, no tracking scripts, and no third-party data collection.
  • No account required: You can use all core features in this release without signing up.
  • Independent: Rosteno is not affiliated with the Fair Work Ombudsman or any government agency.

Expected-pay calculations are limited to verified supported conditions. For this release, automated calculations are provided for supported, worker-confirmed General Retail Industry Award conditions. Other workers can use Rosteno to manage rosters, track shifts and breaks, store payslips, and export records. Where specific award rules are not yet implemented, expected pay will show as Not calculated.
