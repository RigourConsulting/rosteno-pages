Support and Help

Welcome to the support page for Rosteno: Shift, Hours & Pay. Below you will find answers to common questions and guidance on using the app.

1. Where are my records stored?

Your records are stored in Rosteno's app-private Android storage and Room database directly on your phone. Rosteno does not store your information on external servers.

2. Can my employer see or edit my Rosteno records?

No. Rosteno does not have an employer portal. Your employer cannot view, track, or edit your entries. Your records remain completely under your control.

3. How does break recording work?

When you start a break in the Work Clock, you can confirm whether that break is Paid, Unpaid, or Break payment required. Rosteno uses your actual recorded breaks rather than guessing what breaks you took.

4. How do I review payslips after scanning?

When you import a payslip image or document, Rosteno extracts values on your device and opens a Candidate Review screen. You can check the extracted pay period dates, gross pay, net pay, and line items, adjust any figures, and confirm them before they are saved to your records.

5. What is an Evidence Review Pack?

An Evidence Review Pack allows you to export your shift and pay records for a selected pay period. The export creates:

  • A PDF summary formatted for easy reading.
  • A CSV spreadsheet containing your itemised records.
  • A SHA-256 integrity manifest that lets anyone verify the exported files have not been modified.

You can share these files directly through Android's standard share sheet with your union, representative, or advisor.
